Windows will show you a warning. Here's why, and what to do

Aithera is in beta and the installer is not signed with a code-signing certificate yet. Those certificates are expensive and issued to registered companies, so small and early-stage software very often ships without one. When that's the case, Windows SmartScreen shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" screen for any program it hasn't seen many times before. It is not a virus detection — it's Windows saying "I don't recognise the publisher".

  1. Run the installer you downloaded.
  2. When the blue screen appears, click More info.
  3. Click Run anyway.

If you'd rather verify the file yourself first, every release publishes the SHA-256 checksum of the installer on its release page. You can check it in PowerShell before running anything:

Get-FileHash "$HOME\Downloads\Aithera Setup 1.5.1-3.exe" -Algorithm SHA256

Your antivirus may also flag it for the same reason (an unsigned installer that writes a lot of files). If that happens and you'd rather not continue, that's a perfectly reasonable choice — write to us and we'll help.

Your data

Aithera keeps everything on your machine. Uninstalling removes the program and its data. Optional, anonymous technical diagnostics can be turned off in Settings → General, and you can see exactly what would be sent before it goes. The details are in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use.
